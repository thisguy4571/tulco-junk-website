Common questions

Junk Removal FAQ

How is pricing calculated?

Your price is the selected trailer-load fee plus $55 per crew member per hour. Specialty disposal and heavy materials may require separate pricing.

Do I need to move items outside?

No. Our crew can remove items from inside, outside, garages, storage units, and other accessible areas.

Can I text photos for a quote?

Yes. Text photos to (918) 500-9069 with your address or ZIP code and preferred timing.

What materials require a custom quote?

Concrete, dirt, rock, roofing, hot tubs, sheds, demolition, unusually heavy items, and restricted disposal items should be reviewed first.
