Common questions

Know before we haul.

How does Tulco price a junk removal job?

We estimate the trailer space required, then add labor based on crew size and time. Heavy materials, specialty items, demolition, and difficult access are quoted separately.

Can I text photos for a quote?

Yes. Text clear photos to (918) 500-9069 and include your address or ZIP code plus any access details.

What cannot go in the trailer?

Hazardous chemicals, wet paint, fuel, pressurized containers, certain batteries, asbestos-containing material, and other regulated waste may require specialty handling.

Do you offer same-day service?

Same-day and next-day work may be available when route capacity allows. Calling or texting early gives us the best chance to fit you in.

Do I need to move everything outside?

No. Full-service removal means our crew can carry items from inside the property, subject to safe access and job conditions.
